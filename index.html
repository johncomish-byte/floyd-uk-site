About Floyd UK

Floyd UK is the UK’s leading Pink Floyd tribute, meticulously recreating the iconic sound, arrangements and atmosphere of Pink Floyd live.

From the classic albums — The Dark Side of the Moon, Wish You Were Here, The Wall, Animals and beyond — every note, vocal nuance and sonic texture is delivered with precision by top UK musicians.

Expect stunning visuals, the iconic circular screen, lasers, and a production that takes you straight back to the classic Floyd concert experience. Perfect for dedicated fans and anyone who wants to hear the music live the way it was meant to be heard.

The Experience

Every Floyd UK show is built around the full Pink Floyd live experience:

  • Note-for-note performances of the classic catalogue
  • Iconic circular video screen with thematic visuals
  • Spectacular laser and lighting show
  • Authentic sound from dedicated musicians
  • Hits and deep cuts from Dark Side, Wish You Were Here, The Wall and more

Whether you’re a lifelong fan or discovering the music live for the first time, Floyd UK delivers the atmosphere, the power and the magic.
